## 1.0.0

Initial release of Lintel, with four presets: Lintel, Bisque, Neroli and Savile.

- 39 sections, including slideshow, editorial collage, lookbook, shop the look, before / after, comparison table, store locator, material story and customer photo gallery.
- Product pages with eight gallery styles, color swatches, frequently bought together, size guide, subscriptions (selling plans), pickup availability and a sticky add to cart bar.
- Collection pages with filtering, sorting, grid and list views, promotion tiles, and numbered pagination, a view more button or infinite scroll.
- Cart drawer or cart page, with notes, gift wrapping, discount codes, recommendations and a free shipping progress bar.
- Six color schemes, each with a light and a dark palette, and an optional light/dark switcher.
- Predictive search, mega menu, mobile bottom navigation, newsletter popup and cookie consent banner.
- Accessible by keyboard and screen reader (WCAG 2.2 AA targets), with right-to-left language support.
- Translations: English, French and Spanish.
