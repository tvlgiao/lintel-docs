We reply {{ site.support_response_time }}. Before writing, check the FAQ and the support policy. Support covers the theme as we ship it; we can't help with third-party apps or custom code.

Submit a support request

The request form opens on our help center, papathemes.zendesk.com. You'll get an email confirming we received your request, and our replies arrive by email.

What to include

Fill in these details so we can help on the first reply:

DetailWhat to enter
Your name and emailWe reply to this email address.
SubjectA short summary, such as "Mega menu doesn't open on mobile".
Store URLYour store's address, for example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
Theme nameLintel. If you have several copies of the theme, say which one.
DescriptionWhat you expected, what happened, and the steps or page where we can see it.
Screenshots or filesAttach a screenshot or a short screen recording. It's often the fastest way to show the problem.

Never send passwords. Don't include your store admin password or login in a request. If we need access to your store, we'll ask through Shopify's collaborator access, which you approve.
