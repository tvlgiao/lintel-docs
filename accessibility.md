## What the theme does

- **Keyboard access.** Menus, drawers, dialogs, carousels, filters, the variant picker and the cart can all be used with a keyboard. Dialogs and drawers keep focus inside while open and return it when closed. The Escape key closes them.
- **Skip link.** A "Skip to content" link is the first thing keyboard users reach.
- **Visible focus.** Every control shows a focus ring when reached with the keyboard. Its color comes from each color scheme's **Focus ring** setting, with a fallback when its contrast is too low.
- **Screen readers.** Buttons and icons have accessible names, and changes such as cart updates, filter results and form messages are announced.
- **Reduced motion.** When a shopper's device asks for reduced motion, Lintel turns off scroll animations, autoplaying transitions and other motion.
- **Pausable carousels.** Autoplaying slides pause on hover and focus, and have a pause button.
- **Right-to-left languages.** Layouts mirror for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu and other right-to-left languages.
- **Touch targets.** Buttons and links meet the minimum target size for touch.

## What you control

The theme can't check your content. To keep your store accessible:

- **Write alt text** for images in **Content > Files** and on each product's media. Describe what the image shows; leave decorative images empty.
- **Check color contrast** when you edit color schemes. Aim for at least 4.5:1 between text and background. See [Color schemes]({{ '/colors/' | relative_url }}).
- **Keep text off busy images**, or use the **Text background** and overlay settings that sections such as Slideshow and Promotion tile provide.
- **Use headings in order** in rich text: don't skip from a heading to a much smaller one just for its size.
- **Label links clearly.** "Shop the sofa collection" is better than "Click here".
- **Caption videos** you upload, and avoid autoplaying video with sound.
